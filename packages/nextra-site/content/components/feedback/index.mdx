# Feedback

Components that tell someone something happened, or explain a part of
the UI: `Alert`, `Toast`, `Toaster`, `Coachmark`, and the Tips family
(`ToolTip`, `InfoTip`, `PreviewTip`).

Tips live here rather than in Overlays: readers think of them as "the
system explaining something," not something you open and dismiss like
a modal or popover.
