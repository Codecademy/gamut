# Architecture of the system

<Callout type="default">
  **Explanation.** One of the two net-new pages called out in ADR 0001 — largely
  extractable from the old Foundations prose, but not yet written from scratch
  here.
</Callout>

Gamut is split into focused packages: `gamut` (components),
`gamut-styles` (theme, provider, system props), `gamut-icons`,
`gamut-illustrations`, `gamut-patterns` (assets), `gamut-kit` (test
utilities), `gamut-tests`, and `eslint-plugin-gamut`. Components
depend on `gamut-styles` for theming rather than owning their own
style primitives, which is why a single `GamutProvider` at the root of
an app is enough to theme every component consistently.
