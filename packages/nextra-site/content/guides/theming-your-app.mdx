# Theming your app

<Callout type="default">
  **How-to.** Goal: get the right Gamut theme applied to your app.
</Callout>

Every Gamut app renders inside a `GamutProvider` with a `theme` prop —
`coreTheme`, `platformTheme`, `adminTheme`, `lxStudioTheme`, or
`percipioTheme`, all exported from `@codecademy/gamut-styles`. Pick the
one matching your product surface; don't hand-roll a new theme unless
you're building a new product surface (see
[Concepts → Theming model](/concepts/theming-model) for why).
